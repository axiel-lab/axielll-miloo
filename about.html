About

I’m Ishrak Khan, also known as Axiel. I spend a lot of my free time building things, learning new tools and working out why something broke.

Where I started

Discord was one of the first places where I built something people could actually use. That led me into Python, bot features, APIs, databases, and the less glamorous work of keeping a project running.

These days I move between bot development and web projects. I like the whole process: getting an idea, making a rough version, finding what doesn’t work, and slowly making it better.

I’m still learning. This site is where I collect what I make along the way.

How I work

I prefer clear interfaces over loud ones, code I can come back to without getting lost, and features that solve a real problem. Useful first, then polish.

I don’t have a fixed stack. I pick tools based on what I’m building, what I already know, and what makes sense to learn next.

What I’m into

Discord projects
Commands, interactions, moderation systems and music features.
Web development
Clean interfaces, responsive layouts, and making projects easier to use.
Automation
Small scripts and workflows that save time or remove repetitive work.

Outside the editor

Mostly experiments, late-night debugging and unfinished ideas. Some turn into projects. Some teach me something and get archived.
